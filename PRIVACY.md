# Escutaê privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Escutaê is a podcast player. It has no account, no server of its own, no ads, no analytics and
no tracking. This page explains what it does with information.

## What stays on your phone

Everything Escutaê knows about you stays on your phone, in the app's private storage:

- the shows you follow and their episodes,
- where you stopped in each episode, what you've played, and your Up next queue,
- downloaded episodes,
- your settings (speeds, skip lengths, sleep timer, downloads, notifications).

None of it is sent to the developer or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it. You can
copy your list of shows to a file yourself with Settings → Export to OPML; that file goes only
where you choose to save it.

## What the app connects to, and why

To work, Escutaê connects directly to:

- **Apple's podcast directory** (itunes.apple.com), when you search in Discover. Apple receives
  the words you search for and your device's IP address.
- **The servers of the podcasts you follow**, to fetch their feeds, episode audio and artwork.
  Like any website, those servers see your IP address. Some podcast hosts use it to insert
  ads into the audio for your region; that happens on their servers, not in Escutaê.
- **GitHub** (api.github.com), only in the version installed from GitHub releases, to check
  whether a newer version exists. The Google Play version doesn't do this; Google Play updates
  it.

Escutaê doesn't add any identifier of yours to these requests.

## Permissions

- **Internet**: to search, fetch feeds and play or download episodes.
- **Notifications**: playback controls, download progress and "new episodes" alerts. You can
  turn the alerts off in Settings or in Android's settings.
- **Foreground service** (media playback and data sync): to keep playing with the screen off
  and to finish downloads.

## Children

Escutaê isn't directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
